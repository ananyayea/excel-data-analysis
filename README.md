# Bicycle Shop Sales Dashboard – Excel

## Project Overview

The **Bicycle Shop Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, and customer purchasing patterns.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key business metrics, including total sales, total profit, profit percentage, units sold, sales by country, customer segments, and monthly performance.

## Project Objectives

* Monitor overall sales and profit performance.
* Analyze sales distribution across different countries.
* Identify sales patterns across months and years.
* Understand customer purchasing behavior.
* Compare sales performance across customer segments.
* Analyze the distribution of units sold by product or category.
* Present business insights through an interactive dashboard.

## Tools and Techniques Used

* **Microsoft Excel**
* Pivot Tables
* Pivot Charts
* Slicers
* Data Cleaning and Preparation
* Data Aggregation
* Dashboard Design
* Data Visualization

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales and Profit Overview

The dashboard displays important business KPIs, including:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Profit Percentage
* Total Units Sold

### 2. Year and Month Filters

Interactive slicers allow users to filter the dashboard by:

* Year: 2021 and 2022
* Month: January to December

These filters help users analyze performance over different time periods.

### 3. Sales by Country

The dashboard presents the geographical distribution of sales across different countries, including:

* Canada
* England
* France
* Germany
* India
* Italy
* Japan
* USA

### 4. Customer Analysis

A customer-wise sales chart is used to compare the sales generated by different customers.

### 5. Sales by Segment

The dashboard shows the distribution of sales across customer segments, such as:

* Government
* Small Business
* Enterprise
* Other customer segments

### 6. Product and Units Analysis

The dashboard includes visualizations representing product-wise or category-wise unit sales and highlights the top-selling product information.

### 7. Monthly Performance Analysis

The dashboard provides monthly sales and profit visualizations to help identify changes in business performance over time.

* A total of **70,642 units were sold**.

## How to Use the Dashboard

1. Download the Excel dashboard file from this repository.
2. Open the file using Microsoft Excel.
3. Enable editing and content, if prompted.
4. Use the **Year** and **Month** slicers to filter the data.
5. Explore the charts and KPI cards to analyze sales and profit performance.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used to transform sales data into an interactive business dashboard.

The dashboard combines data analysis, Pivot Tables, charts, and interactive filters to help users understand sales trends, profitability, customer behavior, and geographical performance.
